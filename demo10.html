OK, SERIOUSLY FOR A MOMENT. This is an ordinary BareBonesForms form. Nothing about it is special: * The form is forms/demo-retro.json — the same JSON format as every other demo. Text, e-mail, URL, radio, select, checkbox with "other", star rating, and two sections that appear with show_if (pick a 14.4k modem, or admit to FrontPage). * The server validates everything, checks CSRF and the honeypot, and stores the entry in submissions/demo-retro/. Same as Demo 1. * bbf.js and bbf.css were not modified. Not one line. The whole look comes from the <style> block in this page: 1. About 30 --bbf-* CSS variables on a wrapper element (fonts, colors, borders, buttons, errors, stars). 2. About 15 .bbf-* selectors for what variables don't cover (the WordArt title, the pulsing SUBMIT!!!!, the spinning stars). 3. One prefers-reduced-motion block that stops all of it. Even in 1998 we had standards. We just didn't follow them. If a form survives THIS, it will survive your brand guidelines. View source. It's all there. Just like 1998.
